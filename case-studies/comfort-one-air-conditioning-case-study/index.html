The Challenge

A Great Company Held Back by Cookie-Cutter Marketing

Comfort One Air Conditioning, a family-owned HVAC company founded in 2012 by Tapani and Jessica Ojalehto, had been serving the Scottsdale area with exceptional customer service and technical expertise. Despite their commitment to quality work, they struggled with:

content_copy

Cookie-Cutter Marketing

A generic website that failed to rank in Google and didn't reflect their customer-first approach.

no_accounts

Zero Ownership

Their previous agency “rented” them a website for $12,000 a year with no asset ownership.

sync_problem

Unpredictable Lead Flow

The feast-or-famine cycle made staffing and growth planning nearly impossible.

visibility_off

Invisible Online Presence

No local map pack rankings meant losing business to competitors despite superior service.

groups

VC-Funded Competition

Battling large companies with massive budgets driving up PPC and LSA costs.

"We were with a big box marketing firm before and they did not have our best interest in mind. Some of the meta titles and descriptions were for a company in Texas. The reps were not responsive and not helpful."

Tapani Ojalehto, Owner, Comfort One Air Conditioning
The Solution

A Custom-Built HVAC Growth Program

We built a full-stack digital marketing system tailored specifically to Comfort One's brand, market, and growth goals.

1

Custom Website Built for Conversions

  • Authentic digital representation through in-depth interviews with Tapani and Jessica
  • Strategic ideal customer profile guiding how we speak to the market
  • Commission-free techs, industry-leading warranties and same-technician model front and center
  • Scottsdale-area content woven throughout
2

Strategic HVAC-Specific SEO

  • Seasonal content aligned with Arizona's unique climate demands
  • Location-specific pages: Scottsdale, Phoenix, Anthem, Cave Creek
  • Technical work on mobile experience, site speed and Core Web Vitals
  • Review generation driving a surge in 5-star Google Business Profile authority
3

Google Business Profile Domination

  • Full profile audit and rebuild with category optimization
  • Photo strategy and consistent NAP signals
  • Ongoing posts and Q&A management for map pack visibility
  • Consistent growth in views, calls, clicks and direction requests
4

Comprehensive Marketing Integration

  • Database reactivation for duct cleaning and maintenance plans
  • Educational email marketing establishing trusted advisor status
  • CRM integration with Service Fusion
  • Complete closed-loop ROI tracking on every lead
