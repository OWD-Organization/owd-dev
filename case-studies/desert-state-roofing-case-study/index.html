The Challenge

A Great Company Nobody Could Find

Desert State Roofing has served the Phoenix Valley since 2016 with a simple promise: do what you say you're going to do. With 30 years of combined team experience, a 52% close rate, and roughly 36 referrals a month, the team had the skills. What they didn't have was an online presence.

Before this engagement, the business was almost invisible in search. The few people who found them online rarely matched their target customer, 45+ homeowners with $500K+ homes in Cave Creek, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley.

The Solution

A Local SEO Program Built Around Their Strengths

We built a full organic SEO program around Desert State Roofing's core advantages: a premium service area, high-value residential clients, and an exceptional customer journey. No paid ads. No third-party lead services. Pure organic growth.

1

Technical SEO & Indexing

  • Full crawl audit, identified service pages stuck in "Discovered, not indexed"
  • Fixed duplicate content from WordPress tag and category pages
  • Resolved competing duplicate URLs on core service pages
  • Submitted priority pages for re-crawl to accelerate indexing
2

Local SEO Content

  • Rewrote key service pages: local signal audit, competitor analysis, full semantic rewrite
  • Built all 7 service area cities into page copy as geo-targeted signals
  • Optimized for Arizona buying triggers: monsoon season, extreme heat, UV exposure
  • Aligned content with DSR's brand voice: communication, integrity, quality workmanship
3

Google Business Profile

  • Built a complete, local-SEO-optimized profile post content calendar
  • Consistent posting cadence targeting Cave Creek, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley intent
  • Optimized service descriptions, categories and Q&A for Phoenix Valley queries
  • Grew the profile from near-zero engagement to consistent weekly interactions
4

Internal Linking & Site Authority

  • Mapped and implemented internal links from blog posts to service pages
  • Built cross-links between service area pages and primary services
  • Created clear crawl pathways so Google could discover and index every priority page
