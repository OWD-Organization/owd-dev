The Challenge

Trusted Locally, Invisible Online

Exterior Contractor had built the kind of reputation contractors spend years chasing, a 5.0 Google rating, a steady stream of referrals from families across Lincoln, and roofs that held up through Nebraska winters. But search "roofing Lincoln NE," and Exterior Contractor was nowhere on the first two pages. Six months of data told the real story: just 560 organic clicks and an average ranking position of 35.7, page three and beyond, for a business people already trusted.

Underneath that one number were five compounding problems:

Low Search Visibility

Only 560 organic clicks in the prior 6-month period, with an average position of 35.7, page 3 and beyond.

Thin Domain Authority

Few referring domains and little off-page signal to compete with established roofers.

Under-Optimized Site

Service and location pages existed but weren't structured to rank for high-intent Lincoln searches.

Seasonal Dependence

Storm and insurance work created a feast-or-famine cycle without a steady organic lead flow.

No Local Authority Footprint

Limited Nebraska directory, association and media presence to build trust signals.

The Solution

A Four-Pillar SEO Authority Program

Exterior Contractor partnered with Outwork'em Digital in January 2026 with one goal: close the gap between their real-world reputation and their online presence. Rather than chase quick wins, we built a four-pillar program designed to compound, each pillar clearing the way for the next.

1

Technical SEO Foundation

First, we made sure Google could actually crawl and index the site.

  • Crawl and indexing audit, resolved crawl errors and ensured every service and location page was indexable
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals, improved mobile experience and load times
  • Clean site architecture, logical URL and hierarchy structure signaling topical relevance
  • Schema markup, local business and service schema to strengthen rich result eligibility
2

On-Page Content Optimization

Next, we rewrote the pages Lincoln homeowners were actually searching for.

  • Competitor gap analysis, identified keywords Lincoln competitors ranked for that they were missing
  • Service and location pages, rewrote and expanded pages for roof repair, exteriors, siding, windows and garage builds
  • Intent-focused blog content, authority pieces like "How Roof Insurance Claims Work in Nebraska"
  • Metadata and headings, optimized titles, descriptions and H-structure for target Nebraska queries
3

Local & Profile Authority

At the same time, we turned the Google Business Profile into a local authority engine.

  • Profile optimization, categories, services, photos and consistent NAP signals for the Lincoln map pack
  • Local search grid, tracked and improved rankings across Lincoln neighborhoods
  • Review growth, reinforced the 5.0 Google rating with ongoing review generation
  • Ongoing posts and Q&A, kept the profile active to sustain visibility and engagement
4

Off-Page Authority Building

Finally, we built the off-site trust signals that let Google, and homeowners, take them seriously.

  • Nebraska link building, targeted local directories, media outreach and industry association listings
  • Referring domain growth, a steady increase correlating directly with organic traffic gains
  • Domain authority, grew site authority to a 52 domain power score
  • Trust signals, built the off-page footprint that marks a credible Lincoln contractor
