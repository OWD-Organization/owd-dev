The Challenge

30 Years of Excellence Made Invisible Online

Janecek Construction & Remodeling, a father-son company established in 1991, had built a stellar reputation across Northeast Ohio, 30+ years of craftsmanship and 5,000+ completed projects. But a costly website mistake erased their digital presence overnight.

Damaging Website Rebuild

A new site by another agency caused their Google rankings to collapse, costing them visibility and leads.

$4,500 Wasted Investment

Spent on a website that actively damaged their business instead of growing it.

Disappeared from Map Pack

Lost all Google Map Pack visibility for local remodeling searches across Northeast Ohio.

Collapsing Lead Flow

Significant decline in quality inbound leads from homeowners seeking remodeling services.

Outgunned by Large Competitors

Facing pressure from bigger companies with much larger marketing budgets.

"We were in a tough spot. After investing in a new website that was supposed to help, we actually lost visibility and leads."

Matt Janecek, Co-Owner, Janecek Construction & Remodeling
The Solution

A Remodeling-Specific Growth Strategy

We rebuilt Janecek's digital foundation from the ground up, a full-stack approach tailored to the remodeling market and Northeast Ohio's competitive landscape.

1

Values-Driven Website Rebuild

  • Owner interviews to showcase the father-son hands-on approach
  • Trust-building content highlighting 30+ years of craftsmanship
  • Comprehensive service pages for bathroom, kitchen and basement remodeling
  • Conversion-optimized layout built for remodeling prospects
2

Strategic Local SEO

  • Google Business Profile optimization with geo-targeting
  • Location-specific content for Chardon, Mentor, Painesville and surrounding areas
  • Competitor gap analysis to find ranking opportunities
  • Pricing transparency strategy to build trust and qualify leads
3

Remodeling Industry Tactics

  • Project pricing guidelines added for kitchen, bath and basement
  • Portfolio showcase with before and after project imagery
  • Custom conversion-optimized lead capture forms
  • Service area content strategy targeting specific neighborhoods
