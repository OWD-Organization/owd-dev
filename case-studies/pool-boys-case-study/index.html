The Challenge

A Growing Business That Nobody Could Find

The Pool Boys had built a strong reputation over 15 years in League City, Texas. With 300 recurring accounts, a premium service model, and consistent word-of-mouth, the business had real momentum. But their digital presence told a different story.

Before this engagement, their newly migrated WordPress site had no SEO work done on it. Organic clicks were critically low, and the team had no visibility into how their site was performing online.

The Solution

A Local SEO Program Built Around Their Strengths

We built a full organic SEO program around The Pool Boys' core advantages: 15 years of experience, transparent pricing, and a premium service reputation across League City and the surrounding Houston suburbs. No paid ads. Pure organic growth.

1

Technical SEO & Site Recovery

  • Full crawl audit and resolution of all 404 errors and broken legacy redirects
  • XML sitemap submitted and robots.txt corrected to remove indexing blockers
  • Fixed crawl errors identified in Google Search Console
  • Secured all website assets, hosting and DNS control under client ownership
  • SSL certificate implemented with a 28-day automated backup system
2

On-Page & Local SEO Optimization

  • Rewrote service pages for filter service, automation, equipment inspection and weekly maintenance
  • Injected location keywords: League City, Houston, Pearland and Friendswood
  • Optimized meta titles, descriptions, H1/H2 structure and image alt text throughout
  • Conversion-focused CTAs built around transparent pricing as a key differentiator
  • Trust signals: 15 years experience, TX License TICL728, Google rating, Nextdoor Favorite
3

Google Business Profile Management

  • Full profile optimization with updated services, description and photos
  • Review generation strategy driving consistent call and direction-request volume
  • Monthly posts and Q&A monitoring to maintain freshness and engagement
  • Competitive analysis across the League City map pack
4

Content Authority & Internal Linking

  • Blog content optimized for high-value informational keywords driving thousands of engaged users
  • Copy accuracy review: removed claims that did not match actual operations
  • Internal linking improvements connecting blog posts to service pages
  • Structured page hierarchy to signal topical authority to Google
