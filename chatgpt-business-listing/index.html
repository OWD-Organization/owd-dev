ChatGPT business listing help for local companies

A ChatGPT business listing is a profile inside ChatGPT. You can claim it and update the name, services, service area, phone, and website ChatGPT may show. Claiming it does not make ChatGPT recommend you.

  • We help you submit the details OpenAI lets you manage.
  • We line up your website, reviews, and business info so they match.

The listing

What a ChatGPT business listing is

OpenAI opened Businesses in ChatGPT on November 10, 2026. It is still in beta. A listing is the profile for your company inside ChatGPT.

After OpenAI reviews a claim, the owner can edit facts such as the name, services, service area, phone, and website. You can manage a profile only if you own the business, or the owner has allowed you to represent it.

A claim is not the same as a checked profile. OpenAI emails a decision. A separate identity check has to pass before you can publish changes or see mentions and website clicks. The blue check shows up after that check, not from the claim alone.

What the listing is not

It is not an ad. OpenAI says paid ads do not change ChatGPT's answers. It is not a promise of sales, or of a higher spot. ChatGPT may mention a business that has no listing at all.

If the name or address does not match official records, the check can fail. If someone else already claimed the profile, you have to dispute ownership. We will not invent a listing that is not there.

Home services

Why it matters for home service businesses

Homeowners already ask ChatGPT for a plumber, an HVAC company, or a roofer near them. The answer can name a few companies. Your public facts are what that answer has to work with.

People ask for a nearby trade

A homeowner can ask for help in plain words, not a keyword. ChatGPT may reply with a short list. Clear, matching facts give it something accurate to read. They do not reserve a place on that list.

Wrong facts get repeated

If your site shows one phone number and a directory shows another, an answer can quote the wrong one. We line up the name, phone, and service area so they tell the same story.

Google is still the other half

Most homeowners still search Google. A ChatGPT profile does not replace your Google Business Profile or your website. The records should agree. For the bigger picture, read how SEO, AEO, and GEO fit together.

How answers get made

How ChatGPT finds local businesses

ChatGPT search reads the open web. It can cite pages. It may also use other companies that supply nearby business listings. An update you save can take time to show up, and an answer can still draw on something else.

Citations

A citation is another website that lists your business name, address, and phone. When those copies match, there is less conflicting information to quote.

Reviews

Public reviews are a trust signal people can read. Yelp has said ChatGPT can show Yelp reviews, ratings, photos, and business details. We do not claim every review site is used. Steady review habits still belong on your reputation work.

Google Business Profile

Homeowners still use this profile on Google Maps. OpenAI has not said that Google Business Profile, Bing Places, or Apple Business Connect feed ChatGPT. Keep the profile accurate anyway, because the same facts get copied across the web. See our Google Business Profile guide for HVAC and the local SEO plan.

Consistent NAP

NAP means name, address, and phone. Use the same ones on your site, your Google profile, and the directories that list you. If you serve an area and have no storefront, say the service area the same way everywhere.

Website content

Plain pages that name the service and the cities you cover are easier to quote. Say what you do, where you do it, and how to call. That is core website and organic SEO work, and it helps human visitors too.

Schema

Schema is a label in your website code. It names your business, your service, and your area for machines. It does not force a recommendation. We add it on sites we build so the facts are easy to read.

Our process

How we set up a ChatGPT business listing

You stay the owner of the account. We do the parts an agency can do. We tell you when OpenAI will not allow a step.

  1. Look it up

    We check what ChatGPT says about you today, and whether a profile is already there. If it is missing or locked, you hear that before any check starts.

  2. Line up the public record

    We compare your name, phone, service area, and website. We note reviews and pages that disagree. Fixing those conflicts is useful even if a ChatGPT claim is not available yet.

  3. Claim it with matching proof

    You confirm you own the company, or that you are allowed to represent it. OpenAI may send a code to the business phone, a code to the business email, ask for a DNS record, or offer a Shopify connection. If we manage the domain, we add the DNS record. You complete any identity check. We never take your password.

  4. Submit what you can edit

    After OpenAI approves the claim and the identity check, we help you submit the profile details you are allowed to edit. We also check that your site allows OpenAI's search crawler. That crawler is what ChatGPT search uses to read a site. Letting it read the site does not promise placement.

Business Agents, the optional chat on a profile, are for some online businesses in the U.S. We will not set one up unless OpenAI offers it for your company. OpenAI has said that access is planned to grow. It is not part of this setup unless it is actually available.
