ChatGPT listings

Claim your business on ChatGPT. Then optimize it right.

OpenAI opened Businesses in ChatGPT on November 10, 2026. It is still in beta. ChatGPT can already answer questions about nearby businesses. Claiming a listing is a real step. It is not the whole job.

  • Find out if ChatGPT already has your company, and whether you can claim it.
  • Submit the details OpenAI lets you manage: name, services, service area, phone, and website.
  • Make your site readable by ChatGPT search. A listing does not promise placement.
  • Skip the myth. Claiming does not make ChatGPT recommend you.

Why it matters

Registering the name is the easy part.

OpenAI says any eligible business where ChatGPT is offered can claim a profile, then add current information. Eligibility depends on the industry, the location, and OpenAI's own checks. A sloppy claim wastes the attempt.

A claim is not a verified profile

OpenAI reviews the claim and emails a decision. A separate identity check with Persona is required before you can publish changes or see insights such as mentions and website clicks. The blue check shows up after that check is approved, not from the claim alone.

ChatGPT still uses other sources

ChatGPT may use the open web, and it may share a general location with other providers that supply nearby business listings. Yelp says ChatGPT can show Yelp reviews, ratings, photos, and business details. An update you save can take time to appear, and an answer can still draw on something else.

Wrong details fail the check

If the business name, address, or registration does not match your official records, verification can fail. If someone else already claimed the profile, you have to dispute ownership. You can manage a profile only if you own the business or are authorized to represent it.

How we help

Three steps we can do with you.

You stay the owner of the account. We do the parts an agency can actually do, and we tell you when OpenAI will not allow the rest.

  1. Look it up first

    We check whether Business Hub already has your company, and what ChatGPT says about you today. OpenAI says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing, and that not every business can claim a profile. If yours is missing, locked, or not eligible, you hear that before anyone starts a verification.

  2. Claim it with matching proof

    You confirm you own the company or are allowed to represent it. OpenAI may offer a code to the business phone, a code to the business email, a DNS record on your domain, or a Shopify connection. If we manage the domain, we add the DNS record. We line up the name, phone, service area, and website so they match the business you run. Any identity check is yours to complete. We never take your password.

  3. Keep the public record consistent

    After OpenAI approves the claim and the identity check, we help you submit the profile details you are allowed to edit. We also make sure your website allows OpenAI's search crawler, which is what makes a site eligible for ChatGPT search. That does not guarantee placement. If you want us in the account, you add our own ChatGPT logins. Do not forward a personal claim email.

Business Agents, the optional chat on a profile, are currently for eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the U.S. We will not set one up unless OpenAI offers it for your company. OpenAI has said that access is planned to expand. It is not part of this setup unless it is actually available.
