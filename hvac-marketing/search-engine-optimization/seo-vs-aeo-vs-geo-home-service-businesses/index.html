SEO vs AEO vs GEO: How Homeowners Find Home Service Businesses in 2027

Published November 24, 2026 Search Engine Optimization

By Ryan Kearney, Founder, Outwork'em Digital

Your next customer might never see your website. A homeowner with a leak can now find a plumber three ways: Google lists you, Google answers with you, or ChatGPT recommends you. Each one runs on a different habit, and each one needs a little different work from you.

The good news is that the work overlaps. A business with a complete Google profile, real reviews, and clear pages is in good shape for all three. This guide explains each one in plain words and gives you a list of things to do this month.

The 3 Ways Homeowners Search Now

Picture a homeowner whose kitchen sink will not stop dripping. Here is what she might do next.

  • She types "plumber near me" into Google. She sees a map and a list of companies. She taps a few and picks one. Google listed you.
  • She asks Google a question. For example, "why is my faucet still dripping after I changed the washer?" Google may show a short AI answer at the top of the page, with links to the sites it used. Google answered with you.
  • She asks ChatGPT. She types, "Who is a good plumber near me who can come this week?" ChatGPT replies with a short list of companies and a few facts about each. ChatGPT recommended you.

In the first two, she may still visit your site. In the third, she may call from the answer itself. That is why your website alone is no longer the whole story. OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week, so some of your next customers are already asking it.

What SEO, AEO, and GEO Mean in Plain Words

These three acronyms sound like jargon. They are just names for the three paths above. We will explain each one once, then use plain words for the rest of the article: "show up on Google" and "show up in ChatGPT".

SEO: Google lists you

SEO means search engine optimization. It is the work that helps your business show up when someone searches on Google. For a local company, that mostly means your Google Business Profile, your reviews, and your website pages.

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance, and popularity. It also says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking. You earn it with a complete profile, accurate info, photos, and reviews. Our organic SEO services and local SEO program cover this work.

AEO: Google answers with you

AEO means answer engine optimization. It is about being the source when Google, or another tool, gives a direct answer to a question instead of a list of links. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode are the best-known examples.

AEO is an industry word. Google itself does not use it. Here is what Google does say: there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and the basics of SEO still apply. A page has to be indexed and able to show a snippet. So AEO is mostly good SEO, plus pages that answer real questions in plain language.

GEO: ChatGPT recommends you

GEO means generative engine optimization. It is the work that helps tools like ChatGPT know your business, get your facts right, and name you when someone asks for a company like yours.

ChatGPT does not work like a search results page. It builds a short answer from sources it reads. OpenAI uses a crawler called OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search, and says that sites that opt out of it will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers. OpenAI also lets business owners claim a profile in ChatGPT, which we cover below.

Nobody can promise that ChatGPT will name your company. What you can do is make your public facts easy to find, easy to read, and the same everywhere.

What a Plumber, Roofer, or Pool Company Can Do This Month

You do not need a new strategy for each path. These five jobs help you show up on Google and in ChatGPT at the same time. Most take a few hours, not a few weeks.

1. Finish your Google Business Profile and add real photos

Fill in every field: services, hours, service area, phone, and website. Google says businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results. Add photos of your trucks, your crew, and real jobs. Skip stock photos. Our guide to ranking on Google Maps walks through the steps.

2. Ask for reviews steadily, and let customers say what you did and where

A few reviews every week beat a big batch once a year. Make it easy to leave one, and never offer a reward for it or pick who you ask. Reviews that mention the service and the town, like a drain cleared in Mesa or a roof repaired in Gilbert, help people and search tools see what you do and where. Reply to every review. If you want help, see our reputation management services.

3. Give each service and each town its own page

One "Services" page is not enough. A roofer needs a page for repair, a page for replacement, and a page for each town that has real demand. Each page should say what you do, where you do it, how to call, and show a real job. Do not copy the same page and swap the town name. That is thin content, and it does not help you anywhere. Our website design services build these pages into the site from the start.

4. Answer the questions people ask on the phone

Write down the ten questions your office hears most. How much does it cost? Do you come on weekends? How long does a repair take? Put short, honest answers on the matching page, in the first lines under a clear heading. This is the heart of AEO. A page that answers a question plainly is easier for Google to use in an answer, and easier for a person to trust. Only share prices and times you can stand behind.

5. Get listed where ChatGPT looks

Keep your name, address, and phone the same on your site, your Google profile, and the directories that list you. Make sure your robots.txt file does not block OAI-SearchBot, so ChatGPT search can read your site. Then claim your profile in ChatGPT, which is the next section.

If you only do one thing this month, do the first one. Everything else builds on a complete, accurate profile.

The ChatGPT Business Listing

On November 10, 2026, OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT, which is still in beta. Through a tool called Business Hub, an owner can claim the business, update key details, and see how often it is mentioned and how many website clicks it gets. Think of it as a Google Business Profile for ChatGPT.

Here is how it works, in short. You search for your business in Business Hub and pick the matching listing. Then you verify that you own it, using a business phone, a business email, or a DNS record on your website. After that, OpenAI asks for an identity check before you can publish changes. The steps are in OpenAI's help article. A local business can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing, so if yours is not there, you will not be able to add a new one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO helps you show up in the Google list and on the map. AEO helps you be the source when Google gives a direct answer. GEO helps you get named when someone asks ChatGPT for a company like yours. All three rely on accurate facts, real reviews, and clear pages.

Do I need to stop doing SEO to focus on AI?

No. Google says the best practices for SEO still apply to its AI features, and a page must be indexed to be used in them. SEO is the base. AEO and GEO build on it.

Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

No. OpenAI says sponsored placements are clearly labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT's answers, and claiming a business profile does not guarantee a mention. Our $300 setup helps you claim and clean up your profile. It is not a promise of placement.

What is a ChatGPT business listing?

It is a profile for your company inside ChatGPT. After OpenAI approves your claim and your identity check, you can update details such as your services, service area, phone, and website. You can also see mentions and website clicks.

How long does it take to show up in ChatGPT?

There is no set timeline. OpenAI says updates to business details can take time to appear, and ChatGPT may also use other sources. Start with the basics now so your facts are right when it looks.

Do these tips work for any home service company?

Yes. A plumber, roofer, pool company, electrician, or landscaper can use the same five steps. Only the services, towns, and photos change.
