What Is Mailbox Power?

Automated Gifts and Cards That Keep Your Business Top of Mind

Mailbox Power is a done-for-you direct mail and gifting platform that lets you send personalized cards, gifts, and branded experiences to your clients, automatically, on autopilot.

We set it up for your business. You focus on the job. Mailbox Power handles the printing, packing, and shipping of every gift and card, straight to your client's door.

How It Works

A Simple System That Keeps Clients Loyal and Referrals Flowing

1

We Sync Your Contacts

We connect Mailbox Power to your CRM or upload your client list. Every contact gets mapped to the right touchpoint, birthdays, job anniversaries, follow-ups, and more.

2

We Activate Your Automations

Pre-built campaigns go live for client appreciation, referral requests, review asks, and reactivations, all personalized with your branding.

3

Gifts Ship. Relationships Grow.

Mailbox Power prints and ships everything in-house from the U.S. Your clients receive real, tangible touchpoints that no email or text can replicate.

The PRRRR Method

Five Touchpoints That Protect and Grow Your Revenue

Every contractor loses clients silently. The PRRRR Method fixes the five stages where revenue leaks, and turns them into growth opportunities.

P

Prospecting

Break through the digital noise with personalized direct mail that opens doors and starts conversations before your competition even shows up.

R

Recognition

Make your best clients feel seen and appreciated after every job. Higher goodwill means more repeat calls and stronger word-of-mouth.

R

Retention

Automated touchpoints throughout the client lifecycle keep your brand top of mind, preventing churn before it ever happens.

R

Reputation

Consistent, branded gifting strengthens how clients remember and talk about your business, turning happy customers into loyal advocates.

R

Referrals

Systemized follow-up inspires satisfied clients to send friends and family your way, creating a referral engine that runs on autopilot.
