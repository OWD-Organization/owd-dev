Paragon Homes Launches New Brand Identity and Website

Published February 1, 2025 folder Website Launches

Digital Transformation

Outwork'em Digital announces the successful launch of Paragon Homes' completely redesigned website and brand identity. The project delivers a comprehensive digital presence aligned with Paragon's premium positioning in the custom home building market.

Technical Implementation

The new website features enhanced SEO optimization, improved user experience and strategic conversion elements. The redesign includes updated brand elements, logos, colors and typography, that better reflect Paragon's luxury positioning.

Market Impact

The platform is optimized for both organic search and upcoming paid advertising campaigns.

"This launch represents more than just a new website. It's a complete digital transformation designed to attract and convert premium clients."

Ryan Kearney, Co-Founder of Outwork'em Digital
